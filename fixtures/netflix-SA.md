# Plans and Pricing

| Netflix Plans | Features |
| Basic | - Watch on 1 supported device at a time |
| Standard | - Watch on 2 supported devices at a time |
| Premium | - Watch in 4K (Ultra HD) + HDR |

### Pricing (Saudi Riyal)

- Basic: 35 SAR/month

- Standard: 49 SAR/month

- Premium: 71 SAR/month

Depending on where you live, you may be charged taxes in addition to your membership price.
